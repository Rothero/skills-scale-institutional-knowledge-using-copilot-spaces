# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, communication, and execution of software releases. They make sure that teams are aligned on deployment timing, launch quality, and rollback readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Own release planning, launch windows, and deployment checklists
- Coordinate with engineering, QA, product, and stakeholders on release readiness
- Monitor release risks, rollback plans, and post-release verification
- Communicate deployment status, known issues, and recovery plans
- Ensure release notes and stakeholder updates are complete before launch

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment surprises
- Improve operational readiness and stakeholder confidence
- Ensure smooth, well-communicated go-live events

### Typical Communication
- Weekly release readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and release notes
- Launch status updates with product, PM, and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to align releases with milestones and communications
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm the customer and business impact of each release
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to confirm acceptance criteria and deployment readiness
- Escalates blockers to PM or leadership when release risk requires intervention

---

## Product Operations / Program Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Product Operations and Program Operations leads keep planning artifacts, stakeholder communication, and delivery data organized and actionable. They support cross-functional execution by improving clarity, consistency, and reporting.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project planning artifacts, roadmaps, and key status documents
- Coordinate stakeholder updates and recurring communication rhythms
- Track dependencies, decisions, and cross-team alignment across programs
- Support metrics reporting and operational dashboards for delivery health
- Help teams translate execution data into practical decisions

### Goals
- Improve project visibility and operating rhythm
- Reduce communication gaps between teams and stakeholders
- Increase predictability across portfolio and program work

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status reporting and planning alignment meetings
- Cross-functional coordination across product, PM, and engineering groups
- Executive or stakeholder summaries and decision logs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers with planning inputs, stakeholder communication, and metric tracking
- Works with Project Managers to manage schedules, dependencies, and decision logging
- Provides Developers and QA with visibility into scope, timing, and operational constraints
- Helps leadership understand trade-offs and execution health without adding reporting overhead

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance partners help ensure that work meets policy, privacy, and risk requirements throughout the project lifecycle. They provide guidance early enough to reduce rework and support safe delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review project plans and features for security and compliance considerations
- Identify risks related to data handling, access, and governance requirements
- Partner with teams to define mitigation and remediation actions
- Validate secure release criteria and operational controls
- Support incident response coordination when relevant risks materialize

### Goals
- Protect customer trust and organizational compliance obligations
- Reduce avoidable security and policy issues late in delivery
- Make risk management part of normal project execution

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews during planning and release readiness
- Security questionnaires, checklists, and escalation updates
- Incident notifications and remediation discussions as needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and required controls
- Collaborates with Developers to review architecture, secure coding practices, and testing needs
- Coordinates with Release Managers to confirm security gates are met before production rollout
- Provides escalation support when policy or incident risk requires leadership attention

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Enablement leads connect product delivery to customer outcomes, adoption, and support readiness. They help translate customer feedback into product priorities and activation strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Surface customer pain points, feedback, and support trends to the product team
- Help define adoption, onboarding, and enablement needs for released capabilities
- Coordinate customer-facing readiness communications and training materials
- Validate that new features will be understandable and usable in real-world scenarios
- Contribute to success metrics and post-release learning loops

### Goals
- Improve customer adoption and satisfaction
- Close the gap between delivery and real-world usage
- Support healthier product feedback loops across the organization

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and support trend reviews
- Readiness and launch communication with product and support teams
- Adoption and enablement planning with stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Brings market and customer context to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Helps Developers and QA understand real customer workflows and edge cases
- Aligns with Release Managers on launch communication, training, and support readiness
- Shares product impact and risks with stakeholders to support ongoing prioritization

---

## Engineering Manager / Team Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Team Leads support team execution, technical quality, and sustainable delivery. They translate product intent into realistic engineering plans and help maintain team health and focus.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint execution, team capacity, and resourcing alignment
- Help prioritize technical work, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Support cross-team technical planning and architecture decisions
- Guide engineering quality, testability, and delivery reliability
- Partner with Product and Project leadership on trade-offs and execution constraints

### Goals
- Sustain predictable, healthy engineering delivery
- Improve technical quality without sacrificing delivery pace
- Build a scalable and trusted execution rhythm for the team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups, sprint planning, and technical coordination
- Technical trade-off reviews and risk discussions
- Engineering health updates shared with PM and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate priorities into actionable delivery plans
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying dependencies, sequencing, and delivery constraints
- Collaborates with Developers on planning quality and technical implementation decisions
- Partners with Release Managers to ensure engineering readiness and rollback plans are aligned

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles intentionally reinforce accountability, ownership, and clear collaboration paths across product, project, engineering, customer, and operational work.
